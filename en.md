   # Privacy Policy

   Rendezvous respects your privacy. This is what we do, and what we don't.

   ## What we collect

   Rendezvous keeps the following information on your device only:

   - **Big Day settings**: the day you chose, the scene, the codename, the theme
   - **16:8 records**: the start and end time of each session, whether you kept the appointment
   - **Personal notes**: weight entries, mood tags, three-question answers, break-of-fast markers, free-text notes
   - **Preferences**: theme, voice style, privacy mode, whether Live Activity is enabled, and so on

   All of this data lives in the local database (SwiftData) and system preferences (UserDefaults) on your phone. **It never leaves your device.**

   ## What we don't do

   - **No server.** We have no backend. Your records never leave your phone.
   - **No ads.** No banners. No interstitials. No targeted tracking.
   - **No analytics.** No Firebase, no Sentry, no Mixpanel, no third-party analytics or crash SDKs of any kind.
   - **No remote AI.** All copy is selected locally on your phone. We send nothing to remote models.
   - **No HealthKit.** V1 does not integrate with Apple Health.
   - **No iCloud sync.** V1 does not sync any data to iCloud.

   V1.5 plans to offer a "preferences-only iCloud sync" toggle (off by default). When enabled, only theme and voice-style preferences are synced — never body data. We'll ask for your explicit confirmation before turning it on.

   ## Children's privacy

   Rendezvous is not directed at children under 13. We do not actively collect age information, but during Onboarding you may self-declare "under 18 / pregnant or nursing / a history of eating disorders / a medical condition or current medication." In any of those cases, Rendezvous skips the 16:8 timing path — you can still use the Big Day countdown and the home screen companionship.

   If you believe a minor has used Rendezvous without guardian consent, please email us at the address below. We will guide you through deleting all local data on that device.

   ## Data retention

   Your data lives on your device until you either wipe it manually (Self → Erase All Records) or delete the app.

   We have no remote copy, so we cannot restore your data for you — that is the price of privacy. We encourage you to use the **Export** feature periodically to back up important records to your own cloud or local storage.

   ## Your controls

   You can, at any time:

   - **Export everything**: in Self → Export Data, generate a JSON file you can share or back up anywhere
   - **Erase everything**: in Self → Erase All Records, wipe all local data with one tap and return to first launch
   - **Delete the app**: same effect as Erase — all data is gone with the app
